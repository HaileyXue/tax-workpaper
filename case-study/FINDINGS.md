Additional supplied warnings include contractor information returns, due diligence, and uncomputed California AMT. Informational diagnostics stay distinguishable from warnings.

The RSU findings are real packet discrepancies. The independent W-2 box comparison currently finds no mismatch in the delivered W-2s. A regression test introduces a synthetic high-confidence W-2 mismatch to prove the check does not trust confidence. That synthetic test condition is **not** reported as an error found in the original packet.

## Limits

The overlap and FX checks are household-specific rules with explicit IDs, not a general duplicate detection or tax-rule engine. The basis validator is relationship-driven but intentionally narrow: it handles a high-confidence blank basis normalized to zero only when a different supplied document has a numeric adjusted basis with the same acquisition date. Broader checks, depreciation, carryovers, state allocations, and completeness still require further investigation. Resolving one finding does not prove that the return is ready to file.
